# Northstar Analytics Employee Handbook

This synthetic handbook applies only to the FinSyntra public demo tenant. Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave and 10 days of paid sick leave per year. New employees complete a two-week onboarding program covering information security, workplace conduct, and role-based data access.

Employees must not share customer, finance, or HR information outside their authorized department. Questions about leave should be directed to the fictional People Operations team.
