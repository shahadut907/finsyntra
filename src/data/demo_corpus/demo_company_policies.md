# Northstar Analytics Company Policies

Northstar Analytics is a fictional company created solely for the FinSyntra portfolio demonstration. Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Information marked Finance or Human Resources is restricted to authorized roles.

Annual leave requests should be submitted at least five business days in advance. The company observes ten synthetic public holidays in this demonstration dataset.
